Skills editor design study for the panel view; every frame is clickable

Skills you write, inside the panel

An opt-in view beside the conversation, opened like the thread list. It lists the skills the caller may write, creates and edits them (name, description, instructions and the two switches), turns them off, and hands the drafting to the agent. The fields and limits are the runtime's: a kebab-case name of at most 64 characters, a description of at most 1024, instructions of at most 20 000. There is no delete, only off.

Open questions

  1. Where the list lives: a full-panel view (drawn here) or the left drawer the thread list uses, with only the editor full-panel.
  2. How it is opened: a header button, a "Manage skills" entry at the foot of the / palette, or both.
  3. Turning a skill back on: the runtime has disable_skill and no enable. Add an enable to the runtime (drawn here as "Turn on"), let an update carry enabled, or show off skills read-only.
  4. Shared skills the caller cannot write: hidden (drawn here), or listed read-only so a member can see what their personal skill overrides.
